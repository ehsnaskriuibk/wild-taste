# Wild Taste

Rate landscapes in silence, then reveal the locations — see how your taste in nature compares with your friends.

🔗 **[Play it here](https://ehsnaskriuibk.github.io/wild-taste/)**

---

## How it works

Ten landscape images shown without context. Each player silently rates every image — beauty, desire to visit, a location guess, one word. Then the same images come back with the location revealed and everyone shares their scores.

## Controls

- `→` / `Space` — next
- `←` — previous
- `F` — fullscreen
- `Esc` — exit to home

## Settings

Click **Settings** on the home screen to exclude default images or upload your own with custom location labels. Settings persist between sessions.
